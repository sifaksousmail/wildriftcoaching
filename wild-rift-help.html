Wild Rift Help FAQ

What is the best way to get Wild Rift help?

Start with the problem you can see: macro, role habits, VOD review, champion pool, mental, or objective setup. If you are unsure, send one replay for review.

Can I get help without booking live coaching?

Yes. You can read the free guides, start with a free VOD review, or use the PDF guide before booking live coaching.

Does Wild Rift help mean account boosting?

No. Danny's Wild Rift help is coaching, replay review, macro advice, and ranked improvement planning. The goal is to improve your own play.
